AstroByte Scheduler - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This tool collects the minimum data required to function: the OAuth tokens needed to post to connected accounts, and the video content chosen for publishing.

Tokens are stored locally on the operator machine and are never shared, sold, or transmitted to third parties other than the official platform APIs they are issued for.

No analytics, trackers, or advertising identifiers are used. No personal data beyond account identifiers granted by the platform is accessed.

To request deletion of stored tokens or data, contact the operator through the channel official contact links.
